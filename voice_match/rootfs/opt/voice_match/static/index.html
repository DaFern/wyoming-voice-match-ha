Recording, privacy and deletion details

Learning uses about 30 short recordings stored on this Home Assistant system. Your selected speech-to-text service also processes your speech; its privacy and retention rules apply. Only you should use your learning microphone.

Saved recordings are removed after a validated replacement voiceprint is installed. Until then, your existing voiceprint is preserved. Say “Privacy details” in the learning assistant to hear the explanation.

Your signed-in Home Assistant account controls which voices you can manage. Administrators can manage all profiles. Other users can only manage their own.

Deleting samples keeps your voiceprint. Deleting a voice removes both. Neither action removes old backups or guarantees secure erasure from disk.

New microphones or a changed voice may need fresh samples. Keep the original Whisper assistant available as a fallback.

Learning companion pairing

Administrator setup for the separate Voice Match Enrolment (Prototype) integration. This key allows the trusted companion to delegate voice learning as active Home Assistant accounts. Never share it in a chat, automation or notification. It is not the Codex personalisation key.

The displayed key clears after two minutes or when this page is hidden. Copying puts it on your device's clipboard; clear the clipboard after setup. Returning to this page does not reveal an existing key.
