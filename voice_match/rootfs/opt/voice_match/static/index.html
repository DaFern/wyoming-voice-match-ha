Open Voice Learning in app

The app launcher needs the matching Voice Match companion update. It may open your microphone for the initial request, but learning still needs separate spoken consent. Android may remember Voice Learning as your last-used assistant.

  1. Open Assist on this phone and choose Voice Learning. If it has a different name, choose the learning assistant your administrator set up.
  2. Say “Learn my voice”. Listen to the recording notice, then say “Start learning” if you agree.
  3. Read the short sentences at your normal speaking distance. Assist listens again automatically after each prompt.

“Pause learning” keeps unfinished recordings. “Cancel voice learning” discards that unfinished session. Your current voiceprint stays until its replacement is checked and ready.

Can't find the learning assistant? Ask a Home Assistant administrator to finish setup.

Recording, privacy and deletion details

Learning uses about 30 short recordings stored on this Home Assistant system. Your selected speech-to-text service also processes your speech; its privacy and retention rules apply. Only you should use your learning microphone.

Saved recordings are removed after a validated replacement voiceprint is installed. Until then, your existing voiceprint is preserved. Say “Privacy details” in the learning assistant to hear the explanation.

Your signed-in Home Assistant account controls which voices you can manage. Administrators can manage all profiles. Other users can only manage their own.

Deleting samples keeps your voiceprint. Deleting a voice removes both. Neither action removes old backups or guarantees secure erasure from disk.

New microphones or a changed voice may need fresh samples. Keep the original Whisper assistant available as a fallback.

Voice Match connection

Connect your everyday assistant. Already connected? Keep the existing key.

  1. Open Codex Multi Assist → Configure → Voice Match on the Codex entry.
  2. Enter this add-on's internal hostname, port 10352 and personalisation key.
  3. Enable the connection here and save. Use Voice Match as speech-to-text with a Codex agent from that entry.

No separate Wyoming connection is needed. Personal instructions are optional.

Recognition checks (advanced)

These checks affect recognised names and preferences, not speech filtering. Scores are not probabilities. Uncertain matches use neutral preferences and fresh context. Recognition is not authentication.

Voice Learning connection

Set up the separate learning assistant. This learning key is not your Voice Match personalisation key.

  1. In Devices & services, add Codex Assist → Voice Match learning.
  2. Enter this add-on's internal HTTP address on port 8099 and the learning key, not the browser Ingress address.
  3. Enable phone learning and select ordinary Wyoming Whisper, not Voice Match.
  4. Create a Voice Learning assistant with both learning entities and your usual TTS voice. Keep regular assistants unchanged.

This key allows learning for signed-in Home Assistant accounts. Pairing does not start recording.

Key safety

Never share this key in a chat, automation or notification. The displayed key clears after two minutes or when this page is hidden; it cannot be retrieved later. Copying puts it on your clipboard; clear the clipboard after setup.

Recording and privacy

Each person uses their own uniquely assigned profile and signed-in phone. Recording starts only after the spoken notice and separate “start learning” consent.

“Pause learning” keeps unfinished recordings. “Cancel voice learning” deletes that unfinished session's recordings. Both keep the existing voiceprint. Before initial consent, cancellation starts nothing and leaves earlier paused recordings unchanged.

In a verified paused conversation, confirm with “continue learning”. A new conversation, changed login or expired/interrupted session needs the notice and “start learning” again.

Say “privacy details” in Voice Learning for speech-service processing, cleanup and backup limitations. Real phone audio and follow-up listening remain experimental; accountless Echo learning is not available.
